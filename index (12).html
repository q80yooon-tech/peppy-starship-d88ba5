📘 About Resize Image

Change the dimensions of your pictures to the exact size you need for a website, profile photo, form or social post. Enter a width, a height or both. If you fill in only one, the other is calculated to keep the original proportions.

You can also convert the format at the same time, for example from PNG to JPG.

How to use it:

  1. Add your images.
  2. Enter a new width, height or both (in pixels).
  3. Choose the output format and press Process files.

Privacy: Processing happens in your browser, so your files are not uploaded to a Pink PDF Plus server.

❓ Resize Image — Frequently Asked Questions

Will the picture look stretched?

Not if you enter only one value. The other side is calculated automatically to keep the proportions. If you enter both, the image is resized to exactly those numbers.

Can I make an image bigger?

Yes, but enlarging a small image makes it look soft. For best results, resize down rather than up.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Resizing is done on your device.
