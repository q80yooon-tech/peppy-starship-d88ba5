❓ Image Format Converter — Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to transparency?

PNG and WebP keep transparency. JPG does not support it, so transparent areas become white.

Which format has the smallest files?

WebP is usually smallest, then JPG. PNG is lossless and often the largest for photos.

Can I convert HEIC photos here?

Not on this page. Use the HEIC to JPG tool for iPhone photos.
