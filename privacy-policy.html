🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pink PDF Plus ("we", "us") handles information when you visit pinkpdfplus.com. We aim to collect only the information needed to operate, secure, measure, and improve the site.

Files you process with our tools. Pink PDF Plus uses client-side technologies (JavaScript, WebAssembly, Canvas API) to process your PDFs and images directly in your browser. We do not intentionally transmit or store your files on our own servers — everything happens on your device, and closing or refreshing the page removes the in-memory data.

Cookies and similar technologies. Pink PDF Plus may use cookies and similar technologies through analytics and advertising services. These technologies may be used to measure site usage, improve performance, prevent abuse, and, when advertising is enabled, deliver and measure advertisements.

Google Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate website usage, such as which pages are visited and how visitors interact with the site. Google may process technical information associated with visits in accordance with its own policies and terms.

Google AdSense and advertising. Pink PDF Plus may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to serve, measure, and personalize advertising as permitted by applicable law and your choices.

Third parties, including Google, may place and read cookies on visitors' browsers or use web beacons or IP addresses as a result of ad serving on this website. You can learn more about how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.

Your choices and privacy rights. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Depending on your location, you may also have additional privacy rights under applicable law. Where required, we will provide appropriate consent and privacy choices before using technologies that require consent.

Your rights if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland. If you are located in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and equivalent laws, including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent. Where cookies or similar technologies require your consent (for example, for personalized advertising), we will ask for that consent before they are set, and you can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the cookie settings on this site or your browser settings. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Legal basis for processing. Where applicable law requires it, we rely on your consent for non-essential cookies and personalized advertising, and on our legitimate interest in operating, securing, and improving the site for essential functionality and aggregated analytics.

Children's privacy. Pink PDF Plus is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy. We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact us. Questions about this policy? Reach out via our contact email.
