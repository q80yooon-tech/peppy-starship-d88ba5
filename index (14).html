📘 About Split PDF

Pull out only the pages you need or break a large PDF into smaller files. You can extract a range such as 1-3 and 8, split every page into its own PDF, or cut the document into equal parts.

The original file is not changed. Everything runs in your browser, so contracts and personal documents stay on your device.

How to use it:

  1. Add your PDF.
  2. Choose to extract pages, split every page, or split every N pages.
  3. Press Process files and download the PDF or ZIP.

Privacy: Processing happens in your browser, so your files are not uploaded to a Pink PDF Plus server.

❓ Split PDF — Frequently Asked Questions

How do I write the page range?

Use commas and dashes, for example 1-3, 5, 8-10. Pages are numbered from 1.

Can I extract non-consecutive pages?

Yes. List them separated by commas, such as 2, 4, 9. They are saved into one new PDF in the order you list.

Does it change the original file?

No. Your original PDF is untouched and you download new files.

Does it work with protected PDFs?

Files with an open password cannot be read. Some files that only restrict editing may work.
