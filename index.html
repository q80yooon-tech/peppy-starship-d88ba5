❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first file.

Is Pink PDF Plus really 100% free?

The core PDF and image tools have a free plan. PLUS and PRO memberships offer larger file limits and more files per operation. Files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers.

Do I need an account, email or registration?

No. There is no registration, no login and no email required. Open a tool, drop your file, download the result. That's it.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Processing happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API, pdf-lib and PDF.js, so your files stay on your device.

Does it work on Android and iPhone?

Yes. The site is mobile-first — tap any upload box to pick files from your gallery or camera. It also works on desktop Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

Can I convert iPhone HEIC photos?

Yes. Use the HEIC to JPG tool to turn iPhone photos into JPG files that open everywhere.

Is there a file size limit?

File size limits depend on your plan: FREE up to 10 MB per file, PLUS up to 50 MB, and PRO up to 100 MB. Processing happens in your browser, so device memory may also affect performance.
