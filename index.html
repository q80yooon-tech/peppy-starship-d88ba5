📘 About A4 PDF Creator

Type or paste your text and get a clean A4 PDF with automatic line wrapping and page breaks. Pick the font size and margins, then download.

It is useful for quick letters, notes and printable text pages. The tool uses a standard Latin font, so it is best for English and other Latin-script text.

How to use it:

  1. Type or paste your text in the box.
  2. Choose the font size and margins.
  3. Press Create PDF and download the file.

Privacy: Processing happens in your browser, so your files are not uploaded to a Pink PDF Plus server.

❓ A4 PDF Creator — Frequently Asked Questions

Can I write in Arabic or other non-Latin scripts?

Not yet. The built-in font covers Latin characters only, so other scripts may fail or appear incorrectly.

Does it add pages automatically?

Yes. Long text continues onto new A4 pages.

Is my text saved anywhere?

No. The PDF is created in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
