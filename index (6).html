📘 About JPG to PDF

Need to send photos as one tidy document? This JPG to PDF converter places every picture on its own page and gives you a single PDF to download. You can keep each image at its original size or fit it neatly onto an A4 or Letter page.

Everything runs inside your browser, so your photos are never uploaded. It works for scanned receipts, ID photos, homework pages and anything else you have as a JPG or PNG image.

How to use it:

  1. Add one or more JPG or PNG images (drag them in or tap the upload box).
  2. Choose the page size: original image size, A4 or Letter.
  3. Press Process files and download your PDF.

Privacy: Processing happens in your browser, so your files are not uploaded to a Pink PDF Plus server.

❓ JPG to PDF — Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert several JPG files into one PDF?

Yes. Add all the images at once and they are placed in the PDF in the order you added them, one image per page.

Will the image quality drop?

No. JPG and PNG images are embedded into the PDF as they are, without extra compression.

Which page size should I choose?

Choose A4 or Letter if you plan to print the document. Choose 'Fit image' if you want each page to match the exact size of your picture.
