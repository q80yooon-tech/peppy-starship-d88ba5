📘 About PNG to JPG

Convert PNG pictures into JPG files, which are usually much smaller and accepted by almost every website and app. Convert one image or many at once.

JPG cannot store transparency. Any transparent part of your PNG is filled with white or black, whichever you choose, so you will not get a messy result.

How to use it:

  1. Add your PNG files.
  2. Pick the JPG quality and the background colour for transparent areas.
  3. Press Process files and download the JPG.

Privacy: Processing happens in your browser, so your files are not uploaded to a Pink PDF Plus server.

❓ PNG to JPG — Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the JPG smaller than the PNG?

PNG is lossless and stores every pixel exactly. JPG compresses photos much more, so files are usually several times smaller.

What happens to a transparent background?

It is replaced by a solid colour (white or black). JPG does not support transparency.

Should I keep the PNG as well?

Yes, if you may need transparency later. Converting to JPG is a one-way change for transparent areas.
