📘 About Merge PDF

Combine several PDF files into one document without installing anything. Add your files, and they are joined in the order you added them, with all pages kept exactly as they were.

It is handy for assembling applications, invoices, scanned pages or study material into a single file that is easy to send.

How to use it:

  1. Add two or more PDF files.
  2. Remove any file you added by mistake with the × button.
  3. Press Process files and download the merged PDF.

Privacy: Processing happens in your browser, so your files are not uploaded to a Pink PDF Plus server.

❓ Merge PDF — Frequently Asked Questions

How many PDFs can I merge?

There is no fixed limit. The practical limit is the memory of your device, so very large batches may be slow on phones.

In what order are the files merged?

In the order you added them. To change the order, remove the files and add them again in the order you want.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The merge happens in your browser and the result is created on your device.
