📘 About Compress Image

Make your photos and graphics smaller so they upload faster, fit email limits and take less space on your phone. Lower the quality slider for a smaller file, or set a maximum width to shrink big photos even more.

Need an exact size such as 100 KB? Use the dedicated Compress Image to 100KB tool, which aims for a size you choose.

How to use it:

  1. Add one or more images.
  2. Set the quality and, optionally, a maximum width.
  3. Press Process files. The result shows the old and new size.

Privacy: Processing happens in your browser, so your files are not uploaded to a Pink PDF Plus server.

❓ Compress Image — Frequently Asked Questions

How much smaller will my image get?

It depends on the picture. Photos usually shrink a lot at 60–75% quality with little visible difference. The result message shows the exact saving.

Which format is the output?

PNG files stay PNG. Other images are saved as WebP, which is small and supported by modern browsers and apps.

Can I compress many images at once?

Yes. Add them all and you will receive a ZIP file with every compressed image.
