📘 About Rotate PDF

Fix PDFs that were scanned sideways or upside down. Rotate every page, or only the pages you choose, and save the corrected file permanently.

Pages keep their content and quality. Only the orientation changes.

How to use it:

  1. Add your PDF.
  2. Choose the rotation and, if needed, which pages to rotate.
  3. Press Process files and download the rotated PDF.

Privacy: Processing happens in your browser, so your files are not uploaded to a Pink PDF Plus server.

❓ Rotate PDF — Frequently Asked Questions

Can I rotate only some pages?

Yes. Enter page numbers or ranges such as 2, 4-6. Leave the box empty to rotate every page.

Is the rotation saved in the file?

Yes. The downloaded PDF opens in the new orientation in any viewer.

Does rotating reduce quality?

No. The page content is not re-rendered; only its rotation setting changes.
