📘 About HEIC to JPG

iPhones save photos as HEIC by default, and many websites, Windows apps and printers cannot open them. This tool converts HEIC and HEIF photos to standard JPG files that work everywhere.

You can convert one photo or a whole batch. Batches are delivered as a ZIP. The conversion happens in your browser, so your private photos are not uploaded.

How to use it:

  1. Add your HEIC or HEIF photos.
  2. Choose the JPG quality (90% is a good balance).
  3. Press Process files and download your JPG files.

Privacy: Processing happens in your browser, so your files are not uploaded to a Pink PDF Plus server.

❓ HEIC to JPG — Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I open or upload my iPhone photo?

Your photo is probably in HEIC format. Many sites only accept JPG or PNG. Converting it to JPG solves this.

Does converting reduce quality?

JPG is a lossy format, so there is a tiny quality difference. At 90% or higher it is hard to notice.

Can I convert many photos at once?

Yes. Add them together and download a ZIP with all the JPG files.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. Conversion runs in your browser. The first use loads a conversion library, after which your photos are processed on your device.

Can I stop my iPhone from saving HEIC?

Yes. On iPhone go to Settings → Camera → Formats and choose 'Most Compatible'.
